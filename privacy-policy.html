Privacy Policy.

Last Updated: April 2026

1. Information We Collect

3S PETS collects information that is necessary to provide our pet wellness services. This includes "Personal Information" as defined under the **Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011 (the "SPDI Rules")**.

  • Identifiers: Name, Email Address, and Phone Number provided via enquiry forms.
  • Pet Profiles: Age, breed, health conditions, and dietary preferences of your pets.
  • Feedback: Reviews and testimonials submitted through our tail-story section.

2. How We Use Data

We use your data strictly for the following purposes, in compliance with Indian law:

  • To provide personalized pet nutrition and care recommendations.
  • To internalize feedback and improve our "Safe-Formula" product range.
  • To communicate wellness tips and community updates (only with your consent).
  • To comply with legal obligations as required by the laws of India.

3. Data Protection & Storage

We implement industry-standard security practices to protect your data from unauthorized access, as mandated by the IT Act, 2000. Your data is stored on secure servers and is never sold to third-party marketing companies.
