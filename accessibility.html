Accessibility Statement.

Care for Everyone

1. Our Inclusive Mission

At 3S PETS, we believe pet wellness should be accessible to everyone. We are committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities, following the principles of inclusivity outlined in the **Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016 (RPWD Act)**.

2. Conformance Status

We strive to conform to the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 level AA** standards. Our website incorporates semantic HTML, ARIA labels, and responsive design to ensure ease of use across different assistive technologies and devices.
